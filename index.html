⚡ Always-Available Toolkit
Proven techniques — no AI needed
🌬️ Box Breathing (4-4-4-4)
Inhale 4s → Hold 4s → Exhale 4s → Hold 4s. Lowers cortisol within 90 seconds.
🍅 Pomodoro (25+5)
25 min focused work, 5 min break. Prevents cognitive overload during revision.
💪 Power Pose — 2 min
Expand your posture before high-stakes events. Shown to increase felt confidence.
🧊 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding
5 things you see → 4 touch → 3 hear → 2 smell → 1 taste. Stops panic spirals.
